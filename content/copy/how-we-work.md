# How We Work

SEO description: How a Fluigen build works: a free audit, a fixed-price build, a live launch and an optional care plan. You pay the second half only once it runs.

## Intro
Headline: Four steps, one fixed price, and you pay the second half only when it works.
Text: Every project follows the same path, so you always know what happens next, what we need from you and what it costs.

## Step 1: Audit (free)
A 30-minute call with Aliyan. We look at where your team loses the most hours and pick the processes worth automating first.
Within 48 hours you get a one-page plan: three processes to automate, the hours each one saves per week, and a fixed price for each build.
The plan is yours to keep, even if you never hire us.

## Step 2: Build
Week one: we confirm the scope, get access to the tools involved and map the process step by step with you.
Week two: we build it in your own accounts and test it with real examples.
You give us about two hours of your time across the whole build.

## Step 3: Go live
We run the new system alongside your current way of working until it handles real work without mistakes.
Then we hand it over with written documentation and a short video walkthrough, and you pay the second half.
Most builds are live in 2 to 3 weeks from kickoff.

## Step 4: Care Plan (optional)
A monthly plan that keeps everything running:
- We monitor it and fix issues before they reach you
- We update it when one of your tools changes
- We make small improvements as your business changes
- You get a short report each month on what it handled

## What we need from you
- Access to the tools the process runs on
- One point of contact on your side
- About two hours of your time across the build

## Our guarantees
- Pay the second half when it is live. 50% to start, the rest only once the system is running in your business. Not before.
- You own everything. Your accounts, your data, your workflows, full documentation and a handover video.
- 30 days of free fixes. If something needs adjusting after launch, we fix it at no cost.

## Ownership and security
- We build inside your own accounts wherever possible, so your data stays in the tools you already use
- We only ask for the access a build needs, and you can remove it the day we hand over
- At handover you get every login, the workflows themselves, written documentation and a video walkthrough
