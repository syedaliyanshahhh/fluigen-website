## The problem
Every role brings in hundreds of applications. Someone has to read them all, so good candidates wait days for a reply and the best ones accept another offer first.

## What we build
A hiring pipeline that screens every application against the role as soon as it arrives. Clear rules run first, so every rejection can be explained. Then each candidate is scored, assessments are sent and chased, and interviews are booked straight into your calendar with reminders.

A simple dashboard shows where candidates drop off and which sources send the best people.

## What changes
- You only meet candidates who have already cleared every step
- Good applicants hear back the same day, not next week
- Every hiring decision has a clear record behind it
