## The problem
Everything runs through your head. Clients wait for answers only you can give, your team waits for your sign-off, and content stalls whenever you are busy.

## What we build
An AI twin trained on your own material: your frameworks, recordings, documents and way of speaking. Clients or your team can ask it questions at any hour and get answers the way you would give them, in text or in your voice.

You decide who gets in. Access can be tied to a paid subscription, a company email address, or a personal invite, and you update what it knows whenever you like.

## What changes
- Your knowledge is available when you are not
- Clients get help between sessions without waiting for you
- You stop answering the same questions every week

Most builds are live in 2 to 3 weeks. Your audit plan gives a fixed price and a finish date before anything starts.
