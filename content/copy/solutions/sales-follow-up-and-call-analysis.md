## The problem
Follow-up depends on someone remembering, so leads go elsewhere. And nobody has time to review sales calls.

## What we build
- Contacts every new lead within minutes
- Keeps following up by call, text and email on a set schedule
- Adapts to each reply and books in the lead's time zone
- Summarises and reviews every sales call

## What changes
- No lead slips through on a busy day
- Your team only talks to people ready to talk
- Patterns across every call, not just the ones you heard
