## The problem
Leads go cold because follow-up depends on someone remembering. By the time anyone replies, the lead has booked with someone else. Sales calls happen all day, but nobody has time to listen back, so you never really know why deals are won or lost.

## What we build
A follow-up system that contacts every new lead within minutes and keeps going on a set schedule across calls, texts and email. It changes course based on how each person replies, and books meetings in their own time zone.

Every sales call gets a written summary and a review, so you can see what is working without listening to hours of recordings.

## What changes
- No lead slips through because someone was busy
- Your team only speaks to people who are ready to talk
- You see patterns across every call, not just the ones you happened to hear
