## The problem
Your phone rings at 2am, in the middle of a job, or while everyone is on another call. Nobody picks up, so the caller rings the next business on the list. Most people never leave a voicemail. They just move on.

## What we build
An AI receptionist that answers every call in a natural voice, any hour of the day. It asks the questions your team would ask, books the appointment straight into your calendar, and sends your team a written summary the moment the call ends.

If you also need calls going out, we build an AI caller that follows up leads by phone, text and email until they book or say no.

## What changes
- Every call gets answered, including nights, weekends and busy hours
- Callers get booked while they are still on the line
- Your team starts the day with summaries, not voicemails

## Typical timeline
Most receptionist builds are live in 2 to 3 weeks. Week one we map your call flow and write the script with you. Week two we build it and run test calls. Week three it goes live on your real number.
