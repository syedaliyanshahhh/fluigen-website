## What we collect
- Booking details. Calendly, our booking provider, collects what you enter when you book, such as your name, email, company website and notes about your business. It processes this under its own privacy policy and may set cookies on the booking page.
- Emails. If you email us, we keep your message and contact details to reply.
- Analytics. Cloudflare Web Analytics counts visits and page use. It uses no cookies and does not track you across other sites.
- Technical data. Cloudflare, our host, processes standard data such as IP address and browser type to deliver and protect the site.

## How we use it
- To arrange and hold the calls you book
- To reply to you and send the plan or proposal you asked for
- To improve the website and keep it secure

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with
Only the providers that help us run the business, such as Calendly, Cloudflare and our email provider, and only as far as they need. We may also share information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
As long as we need it to work with you and meet legal or accounting duties, then we delete it.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, including the EU, the UK and some US states, you may have the right to see, correct or delete your personal information, or object to how we use it. Email fluigensupport@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

## Children
