# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2026

## Who we are
This website, fluigen.com, is run by Fluigen, based in Chicago, Illinois, United States. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Fluigen. Questions about this policy can be sent to fluigensupport@gmail.com.

## What we collect
- Booking details. When you book a call, our booking provider, Calendly, collects the details you enter, such as your name, email address, company website and anything you tell us about your business. Calendly processes this under its own privacy policy and may set cookies on the booking page.
- Emails. If you email us, we keep your message and contact details so we can reply.
- Website analytics. We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It does not use cookies and does not track you across other websites.
- Technical data. Our hosting provider, Cloudflare, processes standard technical information such as IP addresses and browser type to deliver and protect the site.

## How we use it
- To arrange and hold the calls you book
- To reply to your messages and send you the plan or proposal you asked for
- To understand how the website is used and improve it
- To keep the website secure

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Who we share it with
Only the service providers that help us run the business, such as Calendly, Cloudflare and our email provider, and only as far as they need it to provide their service. We may also share information if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it
We keep booking and email records for as long as we need them to work with you and meet legal or accounting duties, then delete them.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live, including the European Union, the United Kingdom and some US states, you may have the right to see the personal information we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or object to how we use it. To make a request, email fluigensupport@gmail.com and we will reply within 30 days.

## Children
This website is for businesses and is not aimed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.

## Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the effective date above.
