## The problem
A pipe bursts at 2am. If nobody answers, the homeowner calls the next company, and the job is gone.

## What we built
- An AI receptionist that answers every call, around the clock
- Takes claim details and gives callers status updates
- Sends the team a summary and the caller's location on a live map, seconds after the call
- Vehicle tracking, disaster mapping and documents in one platform

## The result
Calls answered 24/7, with the team briefed seconds after every call ends.
