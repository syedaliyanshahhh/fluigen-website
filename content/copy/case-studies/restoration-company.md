## The problem
A pipe bursts at 2am and the homeowner calls a restoration company. If nobody answers, they call the next one on the list, and that job is gone for good.

## What we built
An AI receptionist that answers every inbound call around the clock. It takes the details a claim needs in a normal conversation, coordinates next steps and gives callers status updates without a person on the line.

Seconds after the call ends, a summary reaches the team and the caller's location appears on a live map next to the nearest available vehicles. Around it sits the company's operations platform: vehicle and equipment tracking, disaster mapping and document workflows, in one place instead of a whiteboard and a group chat.

## The result
Calls answered 24/7, with the team briefed seconds after every call ends.
