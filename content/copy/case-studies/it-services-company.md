## The problem
When someone left a client organisation, their logins had to be removed from up to sixteen separate systems, one at a time. Miss one and that access stayed open for months, while new starters waited days for their accounts to arrive.

## What we built
A single portal where the support team and every client organisation work from the same place. Adding a person gives them access to every system they need. Removing them takes it away everywhere at once.

A shared directory shows who has a seat in which system, instead of sixteen separate admin pages. A built-in ticketing system sorts every incoming request, sends it to the right queue, tracks it until it is resolved and confirms back to the person who raised it. Each client organisation only ever sees its own people and its own data.

## The result
16 systems managed from one portal, with access granted and removed in a single step.
