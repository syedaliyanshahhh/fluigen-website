## The problem
A leaver's logins had to be removed from up to sixteen systems, one by one. Miss one and access stayed open for months.

## What we built
- One portal for the support team and every client
- Add or remove a person in every system at once
- A shared directory of who has a seat where
- Ticketing that routes every request and tracks it to resolved
- Each client sees only its own people and data

## The result
16 systems managed from one portal. Access granted and removed in a single step.
