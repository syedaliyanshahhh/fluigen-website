## The problem
The team read every application by hand. Good candidates got buried and took other offers first.

## What we built
- Every application scored 0 to 100 against the role within seconds
- Knockout rules run before any AI scoring, so every rejection can be explained
- Assessments sent and chased by email and text
- Candidates who pass get a booking link and reminders

## The result
9 hiring stages tracked end to end. Nobody reads a resume before the shortlist exists.
