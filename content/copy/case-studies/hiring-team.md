## The problem
The hiring team read every application by hand. Good candidates were buried in the pile and often accepted other offers before anyone got to them.

## What we built
A recruiting engine that carries every candidate from application to offer. Each application is read within seconds and scored from 0 to 100 against the role. Assessments go out automatically, and emails and texts chase completion so nobody on the team has to. Candidates who pass get a booking link, with reminders and confirmations sent on their own.

Clear knockout rules run before any AI scoring, so every rejection can be explained and audited. The team now meets candidates who have already cleared every step, and can see exactly where people drop off.

## The result
9 hiring stages tracked end to end, and the team no longer reads resumes before the shortlist exists.
