## The problem
The coach's AI assistant lived behind a shareable link, so anyone could pass it on and people kept using it without paying. Every shared link was lost revenue.

## What we built
A subscription web app with separate portals for subscribers and for the coach. Billing is connected to access, so it switches on when someone pays and off the moment they cancel.

Subscribers can chat with the twin or call it and hear the coach's own cloned voice, at any hour. The coach uploads new material and the twin learns it straight away. The whole platform went live in about a month.

## The result
0 free riders, with every user on a live subscription. Live in about a month.
