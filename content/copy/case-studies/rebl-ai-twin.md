## The problem
The coach's AI assistant lived behind a shareable link, so people used it without paying.

## What we built
- A subscription app with portals for subscribers and the coach
- Access switches on with payment and off the moment they cancel
- Subscribers chat with the twin or hear the coach's cloned voice
- New material is learned the moment the coach uploads it

## The result
0 free riders. Every user on a live subscription, live in about a month.
