## The problem
The coach's AI assistant lived behind a shareable link, so anyone could pass it on and people kept using it without paying. Every shared link was lost revenue.

## What we built
A subscription web app with separate portals for clients and for the coach's team. Billing is connected to access, so it switches on when someone pays and off when they stop.

The assistant answers in the coach's own cloned voice and draws on a knowledge base the coach can update at any time.
