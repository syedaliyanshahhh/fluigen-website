## The problem
Chasing new leads for weeks needed a full-time hire, and follow-ups slipped on busy days.

## What we built
- 45 days of calls, texts and emails for every lead
- Handles every reply, from "call me next week" to "not interested"
- Books meetings in the prospect's own time zone
- Leads not ready yet move to a monthly follow-up

## The result
45-day follow-up on every lead across 3,000 contacts, replacing a full-time outreach hire.
