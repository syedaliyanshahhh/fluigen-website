## The problem
The agency needed someone to call, text and email new leads for weeks to turn them into meetings. Hiring a full-time person for that was expensive, and follow-ups slipped whenever the team got busy.

## What we built
An AI business development agent that runs a 45-day sequence of voice calls, texts and emails for every lead. It handles every kind of reply, from "call me next week" to "not interested", and books meetings straight into the calendar in the prospect's own time zone.

Leads who are not ready yet move into a monthly follow-up, so nobody is dropped.

## The result
A 45-day follow-up on every lead across a list of 3,000 contacts, replacing a full-time outreach hire.
