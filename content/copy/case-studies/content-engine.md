## The problem
Keeping five social platforms fed with fresh posts, images and videos took hours every week, and content stopped whenever the founder was busy.

## What we built
- One product added to a sheet starts everything
- Scripts, captions, hashtags, product images and videos are created in the brand's voice
- Guardrails keep every post to approved claims, with no medical promises
- The founder approves, edits or skips each post from her phone
- Videos render only after the script is approved, so no credits are wasted
- Approved posts publish to each platform in the right format, with duplicates blocked
- Views, reach and engagement flow back into the sheet, with plain-language alerts if anything fails

## The result
A hands-off content line the founder runs in minutes a week, built and owned inside her own accounts.

## Works with
Google Sheets, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Pinterest
