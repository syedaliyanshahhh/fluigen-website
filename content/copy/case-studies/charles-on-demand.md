## The problem
In a growing real estate company, every question only the CEO could answer waited for his time.

## What we built
- An AI twin trained on the CEO's frameworks and past decisions
- Staff chat with it or call it and hear his cloned voice, at any hour
- One portal for the CEO to manage it, one for staff to ask
- Access by company email, so nobody outside can use it

## The result
Live in about a month, with CEO-level answers for the whole team 24/7.
