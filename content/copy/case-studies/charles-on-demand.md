## The problem
In a growing real estate company, the team kept going back to the CEO for answers only he could give. Every question waited for his time.

## What we built
An internal AI twin trained on the CEO's frameworks, past decisions and way of thinking. Staff can chat with it or call it and hear answers in his cloned voice, at any hour.

There are two portals: one for the CEO to manage what the twin knows and who can use it, and one for staff to ask questions. Access is granted by company email address rather than a subscription, so every staff member can use it from day one and nobody outside the company can.

## The result
Live in about a month, with CEO-level answers available to the whole team 24/7.
