## The problem
In a growing real estate company, the team kept going back to the CEO for answers only he could give. Every question waited for his time.

## What we built
An internal AI twin trained on the CEO's knowledge and way of thinking. Access is granted by company email address rather than a subscription, so every staff member can use it from day one and nobody outside the company can.
