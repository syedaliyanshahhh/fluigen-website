## The problem
Founders running $1M to $20M businesses juggle several inboxes, Slack, calendars, a CRM and their own memory. Every tool pulls them into the details, so follow-ups slip.

## What we built
One screen the founder controls, with an AI layer that reads their tools, flags what matters and drafts the next step. It works at three levels: the whole company, one department, and the exact email, deal or invoice.
- Morning brief with emails awaiting a reply, Slack mentions and prepared meeting notes
- Ask Mimus: questions across email, calendar and Slack, answered with links to the source
- Scheduling: meeting requests spotted, times proposed, the chosen slot booked after approval
- Team access: each person's AI only sees what that person is allowed to see
- Approval first: the AI suggests, people approve, and instructions hidden in emails cannot trigger actions

## The result
Version 1 is in development across web, Mac and iPhone, with Gmail, Google Calendar and Slack connected.
