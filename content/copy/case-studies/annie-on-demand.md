## The problem
Clients need help at night and at weekends. One coach cannot cover every hour, and a week's wait slows their progress.

## What we built
- An off-hours assistant trained on the coach's own frameworks
- Clients chat with it or hear the coach's cloned voice
- Access runs on a subscription
- Coaching support only, not therapy or crisis support

## The result
Support 24/7, so clients keep their momentum between sessions.
