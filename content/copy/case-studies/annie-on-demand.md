## The problem
Coaching clients often need help between sessions, late at night or at the weekend. One coach cannot be available at every hour, and waiting a week for the next session slows their progress.

## What we built
An off-hours support assistant trained on the coach's own frameworks and way of speaking, so answers come from the coach's material, not generic advice. Clients can chat with it or hear answers in the coach's cloned voice, and access runs on a subscription.

It is clearly positioned as coaching support between sessions, not therapy or crisis support.

## The result
Support available 24/7, so clients keep their momentum between sessions.
