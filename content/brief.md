## Audience: business owners

Owners don't buy automation. They buy their time back, fewer lost leads, and one less hire. Every line on the site speaks to one of those.

### What they want

- **Their time back.** They are the bottleneck and they know it.
- **No more lost revenue.** Missed calls, slow replies, leads going cold.
- **No extra hire.** A system that does the work of a role they keep meaning to fill.
- **Done for them.** No learning a new tool, no managing a project.
- **Low risk.** A clear price, a clear finish date, and a way out if it doesn't work.

### What grabs their attention

- **Specific numbers.** "Replies to every lead in under 2 minutes" beats "faster response times".
- **Seeing it work.** A 60-second recording of an AI voice agent handling a real call does more than any paragraph.
- **A real person.** The founder's face and name, and the promise that they talk to him directly.
- **Businesses like theirs.** Case studies named by industry and city.

### What turns them off

- Jargon: n8n, RAG, agents, workflows, LLMs. Tools appear only at the bottom of case studies.
- Buzzwords: "AI-powered", "cutting-edge", "transform your business".
- Stock photos, long forms, and hidden pricing logic.

### Words to use

Use their language: missed calls, leads going cold, copy-paste work, chasing the team for updates, hours back every week.

## The offer

Three steps: a free audit that earns trust, a fixed-price build where the client carries little risk, and a care plan that brings recurring revenue.

| Step | What the client gets | What it does for Fluigen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Free Automation Audit | 30-minute call with Aliyan. Within 48 hours, a one-page plan: 3 processes to automate, hours saved per week for each, and a fixed price per build | The audit plan is the proposal. No separate pitch needed |
| 2. Fixed-price build | One process fully automated, tested, and handed over. Most builds live in 2 to 3 weeks | Clean scope, no hourly disputes, a clear case study at the end |
| 3. Care Plan | Monthly monitoring, fixes when tools change, small improvements, a short monthly report | Recurring revenue and the natural path to the next build |

### The guarantees

- **Pay the second half when it's live.** 50% to start. The other 50% only once the system runs in their business.
- **You own everything.** Their accounts, their workflows, written documentation, and a handover video.
- **30 days of free fixes** after launch.

### How to phrase it on the site

- "You leave the audit with a written plan, even if you never hire us."
- "You pay the second half when it's running in your business. Not before."
- "It's yours. Your accounts, your data, full handover."

### What we don't offer

- Money-back promises tied to hours saved. Results depend on adoption, which we don't control.
- Discounts or "cheap" positioning. Confidence sells at $2-5k, discounts don't.
- Hourly billing.

## CTA and booking

The booking button is visible on every screen. Every CTA takes the visitor to /book, where they pick a time in under a minute.

### Where the CTA appears

- **Hero:** primary button "Book your free automation audit". Secondary link "See what we've built" scrolls to case studies.
- **Under the hero button:** "30 minutes. You leave with a written plan, even if you never hire us."
- **Sticky nav button** "Book a free audit" on every page, desktop and mobile.
- **Homepage:** a CTA after every two sections.
- **End of every solution and case study page.**
- **Final homepage section:** a call-to-action band with one button to /book. No calendar.

### Booking flow

1. Visitor clicks any CTA.
2. The /book page opens with the calendar embedded inline, slots shown in their own time zone.
3. They pick a slot and fill four fields: name, email, company website, and "What would you automate first?"
4. Instant confirmation email with what to bring to the call, plus automatic reminders.
5. Aliyan reviews their website before the call.

### Rules

- **One CTA only.** No newsletter, no chat popup, no "download our guide" at launch.
- **No qualifying form before the calendar.** Qualify on the call, not before it.
- **Booking hours match client time zones.** Evening and night slots in Pakistan time cover US and UK business hours.
- **/book is the single booking page** for the site, emails, proposals and LinkedIn.
- **Tool:** Calendly free plan covers the inline embed, time zones and reminders.

## Homepage

The homepage tells the full story in one scroll: what we do in 5 seconds, proof in 30, and a booking button the whole way down.

1. **Hero.** Headline, subline, CTA button, microcopy line.
   - Recommended headline: "We build AI that answers your calls, follows up your leads, and runs your back office."
   - Alternative: "Stop losing leads to missed calls and slow follow-up."
   - Subline: "Done for you, live in weeks, and you pay the second half only once it's running."
2. **Proof strip.** Three or four real numbers from case studies. Client logos if permitted.
3. **The problem.** "What stops when one person is unavailable?" followed by three pains in owner language. CTA.
4. **What we build.** Five cards, one per solution, each with one outcome line and a link to its page.
5. **See it work.** A 60-second recording of an AI voice agent handling a real call. The strongest attention grabber on the page. CTA.
6. **Case studies.** Three featured, each a headline number plus two lines. Link to all case studies.
7. **How it works.** Audit, build, go live, care. Four steps, one line each. CTA.
8. **Guarantees.** Pay when live, you own everything, 30 days of free fixes.
9. **Who it's for.** Owner-led businesses with repeat processes. Not for: one-off experiments or businesses with no defined process yet.
10. **Founder note.** Aliyan's photo and three lines: who he is, why Fluigen exists, "you'll talk to me directly". CTA.
11. **FAQ.** Who owns it, is my data safe, what if it breaks, how long it takes, do I need to learn anything, what time zone you work in.
